## ABSTRACT

**Background:** The increasing awareness about adverse effects of synthetic chemicals in cosmetic products has led to a significant shift towards herbal and natural alternatives. Herbal face wash formulations offer gentle cleansing while providing therapeutic benefits without causing skin irritation or long-term side effects.

**Objective:** The present study aimed to formulate and evaluate a polyherbal face wash using natural ingredients including Aloe vera, Neem, Tulsi, Turmeric, Orange Peel, Rose, Green Tea, and Tea Tree Oil, and to assess its physicochemical properties, safety profile, and stability.

**Methods:** Fresh and dried herbs were collected, authenticated, and extracted using appropriate solvents. The herbal face wash was formulated using standardized procedures incorporating various herbal extracts in optimized concentrations. The formulation was evaluated for organoleptic properties, physicochemical parameters (pH, viscosity, foamability, spreadability, washability), skin irritation test, and stability studies under different storage conditions following ICH guidelines.

**Results:** The developed herbal face wash exhibited desirable characteristics with pH of 5.8±0.2, suitable for skin compatibility. The formulation demonstrated excellent foaming capacity (82±3.2 mm), good spreadability (14.2±0.8 cm), and complete washability. Skin irritation studies confirmed the formulation to be non-irritant and safe for topical application. Stability studies indicated that the formulation remained stable for 90 days under accelerated conditions with minimal changes in physicochemical properties.

**Conclusion:** The prepared herbal face wash successfully combines multiple beneficial herbal ingredients, exhibiting satisfactory physicochemical characteristics, safety profile, and stability. The formulation demonstrates potential as an effective, safe, and affordable alternative to synthetic face wash products, suitable for various skin types without causing adverse effects.

**Keywords:** Herbal face wash, Aloe vera, Neem, Tulsi, Turmeric, Natural cosmetics, Polyherbal formulation, Skin cleansing, Phytocosmetics
